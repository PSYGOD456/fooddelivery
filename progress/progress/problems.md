# Problems Encountered

## Problem 1 – Requirement Identification

Some requirements were unclear during the initial stage.

### Solution
Discussed the requirements and finalized the major features of the Food Delivery App.

---

## Problem 2 – Database Design

Difficulty was encountered while establishing relationships between customers, restaurants, food items, orders and payments.

### Solution
Reviewed the database structure and corrected the relationships.

---

## Problem 3 – Cart Management

Problems occurred while adding, removing and updating food items in the cart.

### Solution
Modified the cart logic and tested different order scenarios.

---

## Problem 4 – Order Management

Difficulty was encountered while updating order status.

### Solution
Implemented different order states such as Pending, Confirmed, Preparing, Out for Delivery and Delivered.

---

## Problem 5 – Payment Validation

Invalid payment information was accepted during testing.

### Solution
Added validation for payment details and appropriate error messages.
