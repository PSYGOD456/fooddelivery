# Data Flow Diagram – Food Delivery App

## Level 0
Customer → Food Delivery System → Restaurant / Delivery Partner

## Main Data Flows
1. Customer sends registration and login details.
2. Customer requests restaurant and food information.
3. Customer adds selected food to the cart.
4. Customer submits order and delivery details.
5. System sends order information to the restaurant.
6. Restaurant updates order status.
7. Delivery partner receives delivery information and updates delivery status.
8. System returns order status to the customer.
